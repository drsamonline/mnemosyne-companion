# Frequently asked questions

---

**Is this the whole book?**
No. It is the companion app. It carries the study layer — pearls, keynotes, modalities, differentials and miasm groups — and adds spaced-repetition practice. The full clinical descriptions, memory hacks and case histories stay in the book.

**Is it free?**
The app is published for readers of the book. It is free to view and to use for your own personal study. It is not open source and not public domain — see [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

**Can I use it in my teaching, clinic or app?**
Not without written permission. The material is copyrighted. Write to sohilmomin2000@gmail.com.

**Does it work without internet?**
Completely. There are no network calls of any kind. Download the file and it runs anywhere.

**Does it phone home? Does it track me?**
No. There is no server, no analytics, no account and no telemetry. Your progress is stored only in your own browser and never leaves your device.

**Where is my progress saved?**
In your browser's local storage, on that device, in that browser. It does not sync. Clearing browsing data will clear it.

**Why did my progress vanish?**
Most often because you opened the app from a different browser, a different device, or a different file path, or because you cleared site data. Progress is not portable.

**The page is blank.**
That was a real bug in the first build and is fixed in 1.0.0. If you still see nothing, your browser is likely blocking JavaScript for local files — try the online version, or open the file in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

**I only see a plain list of remedies, no tabs.**
That is the built-in no-JavaScript fallback. Your browser or viewer is not running scripts. Open the file in a proper browser.

**Why do only some remedies show a miasm?**
Because the book's Appendix B classifies 51 of the 100 remedies into miasmatic groups. The app shows a tag only where the book does, rather than inventing one.

**Is the kingdom label authoritative?**
No. It is derived from each remedy's source line as a convenience for filtering. Treat it as a rough guide.

**How does the spaced-repetition schedule work?**
Cards move through six boxes with intervals of 0, 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days. Grading "Again" returns a card to the first box; "Hard" drops it one; "Good" advances it one; "Easy" advances it two. Cards reach "mastered" at the fifth box.

**Can I study just one remedy?**
Yes. Open any remedy card and press **Drill this remedy** to run a single-card session.

**Can I reset everything?**
Yes — **Reset progress** on the Study tab.

**Does it work on a phone?**
Yes, and it is designed for it: a bottom navigation bar and single-column layouts. Open it in your phone's browser rather than a file-preview app.

**Which browsers are supported?**
Any current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on desktop or mobile. No plugins required.

**Can I print it?**
Not meaningfully — it is an interactive app, not a print layout. For print, use the book.

**Will there be more features?**
Possibly. A symptom-first repertory search and a "worse at midnight" clock view are the most requested. There is no public roadmap and no contributions are accepted, as the material is copyrighted.

**I found a mistake.**
Please report it — accuracy matters. Open an [issue](https://github.com/drsamonline/mnemosyne-companion/issues) or email sohilmomin2000@gmail.com. Where the app and the book disagree, the book governs.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. It is a study aid. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Read the [disclaimer](DISCLAIMER.md).
