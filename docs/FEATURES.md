# Features

A full tour of every screen and tool in Mnemosyne Companion.

---

## 1. Home

The landing view.

- **A hero panel** carrying the book's kicker line and the promise of the method.
- **Remedy of the day** — chosen deterministically from the calendar date, so the same remedy appears for everyone on a given day. Shows the name, kingdom, family, miasm tags and the Poetic Pearl, with buttons to open the full card or drill it immediately.
- **Your progress** — a progress ring showing the percentage of the 100 remedies mastered, plus started and due-now counts.
- **Three quick cards** linking to the quick reference, a drill, and the diagrams.

## 2. Remedies — quick reference

The reference bench.

- **Search** across names, families, sources, retrieval triggers, keynotes, pearls and differentials. Type a symptom (`worse midnight`), a keynote fragment (`burning`), or a remedy name.
- **Kingdom filter** — plant, mineral, animal, nosode.
- **Miasm filter** — psoric, sycotic, syphilitic, tubercular.
- **A live result count** and a scrollable list; each row shows the remedy number, name, its first keynote and its kingdom.

### Remedy card

Tapping a row opens a side panel with the full study card:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Poetic Pearl** | The four-line verse that fixes the remedy's emotional essence. |
| **Retrieval trigger** | The few words that should summon the whole picture. |
| **Keynotes** | The eight strange, rare and peculiar symptoms that name the remedy. |
| **Modalities** | The *worse* and *better* that seal the choice. |
| **Differential** | How the remedy separates from its nearest competitors. |
| **Source** | The family and preparation. |
| **Chips** | Kingdom, family, miasm tags and the card's current study box. |

Two actions sit at the foot: **Drill this remedy** and **Mark as known**.

## 3. Study — spaced repetition

Active recall on a schedule.

- **Three drill modes:**
  - **Pearl → Remedy** — read the verse, name the remedy.
  - **Keynotes → Remedy** — read three keynotes, name the remedy.
  - **Remedy → Keynotes** — see the name, recall the keynotes before revealing.
- **Self-grading** with four buttons — **Again** (now), **Hard** (1 day), **Good**, **Easy** — each with the interval it will set.
- **A Leitner-style box system.** Cards move between six boxes with intervals of 0, 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days. "Again" drops a card to box one; "Easy" advances it by two.
- **A session of up to 20 cards**, drawing due cards first and then new ones.
- **A progress bar** and a session-complete screen.
- **Reset progress** to start over.

### Progress model

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Started** | Seen at least once. |
| **Due now** | Scheduled for review at or before the present moment. |
| **Mastered** | Reached box five or six. |

## 4. Diagrams

Six hand-written inline SVGs, each with a caption:

1. **The four-phase study cycle** — encode, recall, integrate, master, with the day markers.
2. **The forgetting curve** — Ebbinghaus decay with review spikes flattening the fall.
3. **The four principles of retention** — chunking, elaborative encoding, retrieval practice, dual coding.
4. **Potency scales compared** — the centesimal (C) and fifty-millesimal (LM) scales side by side.
5. **The chronic miasms** — psora, sycosis, syphilis and the tubercular layer branching from chronic disease.
6. **The memory palace** — a house with rooms for grouping remedies.

## 5. Guide

- **The four-phase protocol**, spelled out day by day.
- **A field guide** explaining what each field of a remedy card is for.
- **A short posology note** on reading a potency.
- **A five-minute drill** — a realistic daily practice.
- **The safety frame** — emergency thresholds and the crisis line.

## 6. Study-session timer

Reached from the Guide. A countdown that walks the four phases in order — 90 seconds of encoding, five minutes of consolidation, seven minutes of integration, a minute of mastery — announcing each phase as it completes.

## 7. App-wide

- **Light and dark themes**, remembered between visits.
- **Deep links** — `#remedies`, `#study`, `#diagrams`, `#guide`.
- **Responsive layout** — a horizontal tab bar on desktop, a bottom navigation bar on phones.
- **Fully offline** — no network calls of any kind.
- **No-JavaScript fallback** — a plain static list of all 100 remedies, embedded in the file.
