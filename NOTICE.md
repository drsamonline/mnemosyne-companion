## Miasmatic groupings

The miasmatic classifications shown in the app follow the groupings set out in Appendix B of the book, which in turn follow Hahnemann's three-miasm framework as extended by Kent, Burnett and later prescribers. They are shown for the 51 remedies the book classifies; the remainder carry no miasm tag in the app.
